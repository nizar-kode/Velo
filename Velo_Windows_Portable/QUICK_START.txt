========================================================================
                          VELO QUICK START GUIDE
========================================================================

Thank you for using Velo!
Velo turns your Android smartphone into an ultra-responsive touchpad,
keyboard, and gyro air-laser presentation pointer for your Windows PC.

------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. HOW TO RUN ON WINDOWS
------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. Extract this folder anywhere on your PC (e.g. Desktop or C:\Velo).
2. Double-click "Start_Velo.bat".
3. Velo will start in your system tray (bottom-right near your clock).

Windows SmartScreen Note:
If Windows Defender SmartScreen shows "Windows protected your PC":
-> Click "More info"
-> Click "Run anyway"
(This appears on newly downloaded open-source software that lacks an
expensive commercial digital certificate.)

Firewall Note:
If your phone does not find your PC, run "Allow_Firewall.bat" as
Administrator once to automatically permit incoming Wi-Fi connections.

------------------------------------------------------------------------
2. HOW TO CONNECT FROM YOUR PHONE
------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. Install "Velo-Remote.apk" on your Android phone (Android 8.0+).
2. Connect your phone to the same Wi-Fi network as your PC.
3. Open Velo and tap "Wi-Fi Remote".
4. Your PC will appear automatically in the list!
5. Tap your PC to connect immediately.

------------------------------------------------------------------------
3. FEATURES
------------------------------------------------------------------------
* Glassmorphic Trackpad: Smooth cursor navigation, 2-finger scroll.
* Gyroscope Air Laser: Wave your phone to control the cursor and presentation.
* Media & Key Controls: Volume, presentation slides, play/pause.
* Zero Cloud: 100% offline local network security and instant response.
