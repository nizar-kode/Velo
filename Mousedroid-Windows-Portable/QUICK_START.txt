========================================================================
             MOUSEDROID PORTABLE — QUICK START GUIDE
========================================================================

How to use Mousedroid on this or any other Windows PC:

STEP 1: INSTALL APP ON YOUR PHONE
---------------------------------
Copy "Mousedroid-Premium-v2.apk" from this folder to your Android phone,
or download it on your phone, then open and install it.

STEP 2: RUN SERVER ON THIS PC
-----------------------------
Simply double-click "Start_Mousedroid.bat".
- No installation required.
- No Python required.
- Everything runs natively from this portable folder.
- You will see the Mousedroid icon appear in the Windows System Tray
  near the clock.

STEP 3: 1-TAP CONNECT
---------------------
1. Ensure your phone is connected to the same Wi-Fi network as this PC.
2. Open Mousedroid on your phone.
3. Tap "Wi-Fi Remote (Auto-Connect)".
4. This PC will appear AUTOMATICALLY in the device list!
5. Tap your PC to connect. That's it!

------------------------------------------------------------------------
FIREWALL NOTICE (IF DEVICES DON'T APPEAR):
------------------------------------------------------------------------
If your phone doesn't discover this PC automatically, right-click
"Allow_Firewall.bat" and choose "Run as administrator" to permit ports
48291 and 48292 through Windows Firewall.
