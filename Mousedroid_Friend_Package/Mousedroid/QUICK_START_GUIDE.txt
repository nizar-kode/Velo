========================================================================
                     MOUSEDROID REMOTE — QUICK START
========================================================================

Welcome to Mousedroid! Turn your phone into a wireless trackpad and
presentation remote for your computer.

------------------------------------------------------------------------
STEP 1: PHONE (Takes 30 seconds)
------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. Go into the folder "1_ANDROID_PHONE".
2. Send "Mousedroid.apk" to your Android phone and install it.

------------------------------------------------------------------------
STEP 2: COMPUTER (Takes 5 seconds)
------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. Go into the folder "2_WINDOWS_PC".
2. Double-click "START_MOUSEDROID.bat".
   (No installation or software required!)

------------------------------------------------------------------------
STEP 3: CONNECT
------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. Ensure your phone and PC are on the SAME Wi-Fi network.
2. Open the Mousedroid app on your phone.
3. Tap "Wi-Fi Remote (Auto-Connect)".
4. Your PC's name will appear automatically! Tap it to connect.

Enjoy using your phone as a trackpad and remote control!
