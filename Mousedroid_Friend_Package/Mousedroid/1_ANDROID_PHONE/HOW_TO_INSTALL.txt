============================================================
           HOW TO INSTALL MOUSEDROID ON YOUR PHONE
============================================================

1. SEND "Mousedroid.apk" TO YOUR PHONE
   - You can send it via WhatsApp, Telegram, Google Drive,
     or connect your phone via USB and copy it over.

2. TAP "Mousedroid.apk" TO INSTALL
   - If your phone asks "Install unknown apps", tap Allow.

3. OPEN MOUSEDROID
   - Connect to the same Wi-Fi as your PC.
   - Tap "Wi-Fi Remote (Auto-Connect)".
   - Your PC will appear automatically! Tap it to connect.
