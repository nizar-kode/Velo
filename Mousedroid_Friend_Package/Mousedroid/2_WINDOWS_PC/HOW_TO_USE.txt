============================================================
              HOW TO USE MOUSEDROID ON PC
============================================================

1. DOUBLE-CLICK "START_MOUSEDROID.bat"
   - No installation needed.
   - A dark icon will appear in your system tray near the clock.

2. OPEN THE APP ON YOUR PHONE
   - Make sure your phone is on the same Wi-Fi as this PC.
   - Tap "Wi-Fi Remote (Auto-Connect)".
   - Tap this PC when it shows up!

------------------------------------------------------------
TROUBLESHOOTING (IF PC DOESN'T APPEAR ON PHONE):
------------------------------------------------------------
If your phone doesn't see your PC:
- Right-click "FIREWALL_HELPER_IF_BLOCKED.bat"
- Choose "Run as administrator"
- Then run "START_MOUSEDROID.bat" again.
